Pediatric ER Companion v1.3.1

Weight-based paediatric emergency protocols at the bedside.

What it does

Step-by-step protocols for common paediatric emergencies — status epilepticus, anaphylaxis, DKA, septic and dengue shock, snake bite, poisoning, head injury, burns, meningitis and more — with every dose calculated from the child's weight and capped at adult maximums.

  • Enter a measured weight, or estimate it from age (clearly flagged as an estimate).
  • Doses show the calculated amount, the per-kg rule, route and key cautions; infusions show how to make them up.
  • A drug library of common ER drugs with stock strengths, draw-up volumes and concentration checks.
  • Scores and calculators: paediatric GCS, PEWS, PRAM, Westley, WHO dehydration, PECARN head injury, burns fluids, maintenance fluids and normal vital signs.
  • Voice commands and read-aloud, Night / Day / Bright (sunlight) modes, adjustable text size, and phone, tablet and landscape layouts.
  • Works offline once opened, and can be installed to the home screen.

Sources

Protocols are aligned to the IAP Standard Treatment Guidelines, WHO guidance and AHA PALS 2020, with condition-specific guidelines (ISPAD, Surviving Sepsis Campaign, MoHFW snakebite protocol and others) cited on each protocol page.
