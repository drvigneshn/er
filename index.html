Disclaimer

A decision-support aid for trained clinicians. It calculates weight-based doses aligned with IAP STG, WHO and AHA PALS 2020.

Independently verify every dose, volume and rate before giving it. This tool aids recall and calculation only — it is not a medical device and does not replace clinical judgment. The treating clinician remains fully responsible.

Scope: infants and children; doses capped at adult maximums. Not for neonatal (NRP / NICU) care. Nothing you enter leaves this device.

Verify every dose before administration.

How to use

  1. Enter the weight — type it, or pick an age for an estimate (marked "Estimated").
  2. Find what you need — search, or browse All, Protocols, Drugs or Scores.
  3. Read a dose card — the big number is the dose for this child; below it are the per-kg rule, maximum and route. "= X ml of …" is the volume to draw up for that strength.
  4. Check the strength — amber "Concentration check" boxes flag look-alike ampoules.
  5. Scores — tap one answer per item; the result appears when all are answered.
  6. Voice — tap the mic, then say "open DKA", "weight 12", "read", "next" or "stop".
  7. Menu (≡) — text size, layout (Mobile / Tablet / Landscape) and colour mode.
  8. New patient — clears the weight and answers. Patient details are kept only while the app is open.
  9. Install — browser menu → "Add to Home screen". Works offline after the first open.
