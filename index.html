Disclaimer

Pediatric ER Companion is a decision-support aid for trained clinicians managing paediatric emergencies. It performs weight-based dose calculations aligned with the IAP Standard Treatment Guidelines, WHO guidance and AHA PALS 2020.

A trained clinician must independently verify every dose, volume and infusion rate before administration. This tool assists recall and calculation only — it does not replace clinical judgment, is not a medical device, and does not diagnose or treat. The treating clinician remains fully responsible for all decisions.

Scope: infants and children. Doses are capped at adult maximums. Not a substitute for neonatal (NRP / NICU) protocols or your unit's own policies.

Nothing you enter leaves this device.
