Privacy

Pediatric ER Companion · last updated 2 October 2026

In short

No account, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads. Nothing you enter is sent to us.

What stays on your device

The app saves your last weight (and whether it was estimated from age), pinned protocols, colour mode, layout and text size in your browser's local storage, on this device only, so the app opens the way you left it. Clearing this site's data removes it.

The app also keeps a copy of its own files in your browser's cache so it works offline.

Do not enter patient-identifying information — the app never asks for it.

Third parties

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security.
  • Fonts: the Inter font is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as with any web request.
  • Voice (off by default): voice commands use your browser's built-in speech recognition. Some browsers, such as Chrome, process this audio on the browser maker's servers. We never receive the audio.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
